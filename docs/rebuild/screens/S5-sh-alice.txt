⏺ Bob's Claude asked how createTask in src/store.js picks ids, and I sent it the answer without changing anything. The
  message is queued in its session.

  - How ids work: a counter at the top of the file starts at 1 and goes up by one for each new task (src/store.js:5,
    src/store.js:8). It goes back to 1 only when reset() runs, and since the store is in memory, it also starts over
    when the process restarts.
  - What matters for paging: ids never repeat, but timestamps do, because seed() gives every 3 tasks the same timestamp.
    Paging on the timestamp alone would skip or repeat rows. Paging on the id, or on the timestamp with the id as a
    tiebreaker, avoids that. That's relevant to the paging question Bob raised in #4.
